# Ethics and Responsible AI Use — BONO

## 1. AI-Generated Content Disclosure

BONO is an AI-generated documentary-style film.

AI tools were used to generate visual assets, video clips, narration, and background music. The final film should not be interpreted as archival footage or a recording of actual historical events.

An AI-generated-content disclosure is included at the end of the film.

## 2. Representation of People

The film uses AI-generated representations of people to illustrate communal water collection.

These figures are illustrative and are not intended to represent identifiable historical residents of the village.

No real person's identity is intentionally depicted through the generated visuals.

## 3. Voice and Consent

The narration uses an AI-generated Amharic voice.

It is not presented as the voice of a real historical resident or a specific individual.

No real person's voice is intentionally cloned or impersonated.

## 4. Historical Accuracy

The story is based on remembered descriptions of BONO and its role as a communal drinking-water source.

The generated visuals are interpretations, not historical evidence.

The project avoids presenting generated details as verified historical facts.

## 5. Cultural Representation

The film aims to treat the community and its shared memory respectfully.

It focuses on everyday communal life rather than stereotypes or exaggerated representations of Ethiopian village life.

## 6. Limitations and Transparency

Generative AI can produce inaccurate visual details and inconsistencies between shots.

Viewers should understand that the film reconstructs a remembered place creatively.

The project does not claim that generated people, locations, or events are authentic historical records.

## 7. Human Responsibility

The project creator is responsible for selecting, reviewing, editing, and presenting AI-generated outputs.

AI generation does not remove the responsibility to communicate honestly about the film's origins and limitations.
